KiloWatch

A personal program that watches the electricity use of one home.

KiloWatch is a small program for one person's own computer. It puts together the home's electricity use from Hydro-Québec, the weather, a spa logger and meters read by hand, and it reads the thermostat in the same home through Google's Nest Device Access API, to show how much of the use comes from heating.

It is not a product, it has no accounts and no other users, and it is not offered to anyone else. It runs on the owner's own PC and keeps its data there.

What it asks Google for: read access to the owner's own Nest thermostat (temperature, humidity, heating state, target temperature) and access to the owner's own Pub/Sub subscription, so that changes of the thermostat arrive as they happen. See the privacy policy for how that information is used.
