KiloWatch: privacy policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026. Back to the home page.

KiloWatch is a personal program that its owner runs on his own computer. The only person whose Google account it ever uses is the owner. It is not offered to other people.

What information it reads from Google

  • From the owner's Google Nest thermostat, through Google's Smart Device Management (Device Access) API: the room temperature, the humidity, whether the system is heating, the target temperature, the mode and whether the thermostat is online.
  • From the owner's own Google Cloud Pub/Sub subscription: the messages Google publishes when those thermostat values change.

It does not read cameras, doorbells, emails, files, contacts or anything else in the Google account.

How the information is used and stored

The values are used only to show the owner, on his own computer and on his own home network, how much of his home's electricity use goes to heating. They are kept in files on the owner's computer. The sign-in permission Google gives (a token) is stored on that computer, protected by Windows.

What is not done with it

The information is not sent to any server of the program, not shared with anyone, not sold, not used for advertising and not used to train any model. No person other than the owner reads it.

Google API Services User Data Policy

KiloWatch's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and deleting data

The owner can remove KiloWatch's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions or in the Partner Connections Manager at nestservices.google.com/partnerconnections. Deleting the program's data folder on the computer deletes everything it has stored.
